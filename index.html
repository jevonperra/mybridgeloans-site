Loan programs

When a short timeline and certainty of close are important.

  • 1st trust deed bridge

    Purchase or refinance while the borrower sells, refinances, or stabilizes. Single-family through small multifamily.

  • 2nd trust deed

    Pull equity behind a current first without touching the first. Not offered in Texas.

  • Commercial

    Retail, office, industrial, mixed-use. Bridge to sale, lease-up, or permanent financing.

  • Owner occupied hard money

    Bridge financing on a home the borrower lives in. The loan still needs a business purpose. Tell us the use.

  • Home loans in California

    Conventional, FHA, VA and non-QM for a home you live in. Thirty-year fixed and everything in between.

Home loans in California

For a home you live in, we broker the standard programs and coordinate most financing transactions.

  • Conventional Fixed and adjustable, including thirty-year fixed. Purchase and refinance.
  • FHA Lower down payment and flexible credit for primary residences.
  • VA For eligible veterans and service members. No down payment on most purchases.
  • Non-QM Bank statement, asset-based, and other income documentation outside the standard box.
