Tax lien payoff loan secured by real estate

MyBridge Loans funds private money loans that pay off IRS, state, and county tax liens on non-owner-occupied real estate. The lien is paid directly through escrow and we record a trust deed on the property. Up to 65% loan-to-value. No minimum FICO, no income documentation. Same-day answer on any scenario. Typical funding in two weeks.

The problem a tax lien creates

A recorded tax lien attaches to the property and sits ahead of any new loan. Banks will not lend behind it, and a sale or refinance cannot close until it is paid. Meanwhile penalties and interest keep running. A private money loan solves the order-of-operations problem: the loan pays the lien at closing, the lien is released, and the new trust deed records clean.

How the closing works

  • You send the address, value, the lien amount, and anything else owed on the property.
  • We issue terms the same day.
  • Title orders the payoff demand from the IRS, Franchise Tax Board, or county.
  • At funding, escrow pays the lien from loan proceeds. The release records and our trust deed records.
  • Any remaining proceeds go to you for a business purpose.

What we need to say yes

  • Non-owner-occupied property: a rental, a second home, land with a plan, or commercial.
  • Enough equity that the lien, any existing loan, and our loan together stay inside 65% of value. Cross a second property if not.
  • An exit: sale, refinance, or a business event that pays us off.
  • If there is an existing first mortgage, it stays current or gets paid off in the same closing.

Terms

1st trust deeds 9.5% to 10.99% and up; 2nd trust deeds 11.99% to 12.99% and up. Interest-only payments. Usually 1.5 to 2 lender points plus broker, 3-year note, 3 to 6 month prepayment penalty. Loans from $100,000 to $4,000,000 and up. Underwriting $1,995, no processing fee, third-party appraisal, title, escrow and credit at cost. Exceptions available.

Owner-occupied property

A tax lien on the home you live in can still be handled if the loan itself has a business purpose, for example when the lien arose from business taxes. See owner-occupied business purpose loans. If the purpose is personal, it is a consumer loan, which we broker in California only.
