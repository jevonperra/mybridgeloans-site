Common questions

Where has My Bridge Loans funded loans?

Most funded loans are in California, across Orange County, Los Angeles, San Diego, the Inland Empire, and the Bay Area. Others are in Washington, Florida, Hawaii, Colorado, Arizona, Texas, Tennessee, Michigan, and South Carolina.

What loan-to-value do funded loans close at?

Where loan-to-value is published on this page, 1st trust deeds closed between 11% and 70%, most at 65% or less. Second trust deeds closed between 36% and 65.73% combined loan-to-value. The standard limits are 65% on a 1st and usually 60% combined on a 2nd; anything higher was an exception.

Why are there no addresses or borrower names?

To protect borrowers. My Bridge Loans publishes only the city, state, loan amount, property type, and loan-to-value.
