Hard money 2nd trust deed in Orange County, CA

MyBridge Loans funds private money second trust deeds in Orange County and Los Angeles up to 65% combined loan-to-value, behind an existing first mortgage that stays in place. Business purpose only. Same-day answer on any scenario.

How the numbers work

Take the as-is value, multiply by 65%, subtract the first mortgage balance. That is the maximum second. Example: $1,200,000 value × 65% = $780,000. First mortgage of $500,000. Maximum 2nd trust deed: $280,000. Outside Southern California the cap is about 5% lower.

What we need to say yes

  • The first mortgage is current, with no lates in the last 12 months and no forbearance.
  • The use of funds is a business purpose: a business, an investment property, business debt, or taxes tied to a business or investment.
  • A clear exit: sale, refinance, or a business event that pays the loan off.
  • Clean title. Tax liens and judgments can usually be paid through escrow.

What does not matter much

FICO, income documentation, and self-employment. The loan is priced on the position and the equity. A higher score prices better, but a lower score with strong equity and a clean first-mortgage history still funds.

Cost

Interest-only, priced on lien position, loan-to-value, property, and exit. There is one broker fee in the deal because we fund through our own investors.

Common uses

  • Down payment on an investment property or a business acquisition.
  • Paying an IRS or FTB lien so a sale or refinance can close.
  • Buying out a partner, sibling, or ex-spouse's interest in a rental.
  • Working capital against a rental or commercial building with a low-rate first you don't want to lose.

How to get a quote

Send the address, value, first mortgage balance, loan amount, FICO, occupancy, use of funds, and exit to the scenario form. You get a yes, no, or what's missing the same day.
