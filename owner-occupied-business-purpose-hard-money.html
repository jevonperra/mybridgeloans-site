Owner-occupied business purpose hard money loan

MyBridge Loans funds hard money loans on a home the borrower lives in, when the money is for a business purpose. 1st trust deed up to 65% loan-to-value, or 2nd trust deed usually up to 60% combined. No minimum FICO, no income documentation. Same-day answer on any scenario. Typical funding in two weeks.

Why most lenders say no to this

Most private lenders will not touch an owner-occupied home at all, because a consumer loan on a primary residence carries a heavy compliance load. The way through is the purpose of the loan. When the funds go to a business or investment use, the loan is a business loan, even though the collateral is the house you sleep in. We fund those.

What qualifies as business purpose

  • Down payment or purchase of an investment property.
  • Capital for a business you own, or buying into one.
  • Buying out a business partner.
  • Paying business taxes or business debt.
  • Inventory, equipment, or a commercial lease deposit.

The test is where the majority of the money goes. Paying off personal credit cards, a car, a wedding, or tuition is personal, and that is a consumer loan. Tell us the use up front. We will tell you which side it falls on.
